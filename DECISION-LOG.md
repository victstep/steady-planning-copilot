# Decision Log — Working with AI

## Accepted suggestions

1. **Explicit plan confirmation.** This keeps the human in control and distinguishes Steady from autonomous agents.
2. **Optional capacity check-in.** A user-selected pace can make a plan realistic without claiming to assess mental health.

## Revised suggestions

1. **Automatic deadlines for every task** became editable suggested timing because the AI lacks context to make commitments for users.
2. **Equal visual weight for every task** became one highlighted “Start here” action for low-energy days.

## Rejected suggestion

**Infer mental state from wording or task behaviour.** Rejected because it is unreliable, creates privacy and trust risks, and expands the concept into mental-health assessment rather than planning support.
