# Project Brief — Steady

## Overview

Steady is a human-controlled AI planning copilot. It turns an unstructured responsibility or brain dump into a clear, editable daily plan with minimal effort. It proposes; the user reviews, changes, confirms, and acts.

## Problem

People manage responsibilities across memory, notes, messages, and calendars. Traditional to-do apps make them manually structure, prioritise, and date every task before they can begin.

## Primary user

People juggling work, school, home, and personal commitments who feel overwhelmed by organising them.

## Primary task

Turn a messy thought or request into an actionable plan with minimal effort.

## Prototype question

Can users capture, organise, and act on responsibilities more easily with an AI copilot than with a traditional to-do app?

## Success criteria

- The user goes from raw text to a reviewed plan in one short flow.
- The user does less manual structuring than in a traditional to-do app.
- No task is committed without explicit confirmation.
- A low-energy choice produces a smaller, more manageable plan.

## Scope

**In scope:** Natural-language input, simulated AI task suggestions, editable priorities, optional capacity selection, confirmation, completion, responsive layout, and a Miro-inspired visual system adapted for individual planning.

**Out of scope:** Live AI, email/calendar/finance integrations, purchases, bookings, autonomous actions, mental-state inference, medical advice, and mood tracking.

## Prototype limitation

AI output is scripted prototype content rather than a live model. It demonstrates the interaction without claiming real-world decision accuracy.
